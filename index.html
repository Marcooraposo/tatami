The engine

Three things you already have, finally working together

Your curriculum, your student records, and your class history are usually scattered across notebooks, spreadsheets, and memory. openmat.me joins them — and turns them into insight you can act on.

📘

Curriculum

Techniques, syllabus, and the path from white to black belt.

👤

Student data

Belts, stripes, goals, injuries, and history for every member.

📅

Class history

Who trained what, when — attendance and what was covered.

delivers

Progress insight

See exactly where each student stands, what they've covered, and who's ready to be promoted — objectively, not from memory.

Smarter class management

openmat.me sees what your room has and hasn't covered — and who's been away — then flags gaps and suggests where to take the next class.

Objective tailoring

Give each student a clear next step suited to their level — so progress feels personal, not one-size-fits-all.

The result: students who feel they're improving stay engaged, stay motivated, and keep coming back — driving retention across the board.

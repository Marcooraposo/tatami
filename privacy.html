Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026

This policy explains what information openmat.me collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain because that reflects how we handle your data.

Who we are

openmat.me ("openmat", "we", "us") is a forthcoming academy-management platform built for jiu-jitsu and other martial arts academies. This website is currently a marketing and waitlist site. You can reach us at hello@openmat.me.

What we collect

  • Waitlist details. When you join the waitlist, we collect the information you submit — typically your email address, and optionally details such as your academy name or discipline.
  • Basic usage data. Like most websites, our host records standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps) to operate and secure the site.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information through this website, and we do not collect information from children.

How we use it

  • To contact you about early access and the launch of openmat.me.
  • To respond to enquiries you send us.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your waitlist email for unrelated marketing.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of third parties to run this site. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Tally — powers the waitlist form and stores the details you submit. See Tally's privacy policy at tally.so.
  • Vercel — hosts the website and processes standard server logs. See Vercel's privacy policy at vercel.com.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on the site, which may involve a request to Google's servers.

Cookies

This site does not set marketing or tracking cookies. We store a single preference in your browser's local storage to remember your chosen language. Our service providers may set cookies that are strictly necessary to deliver their features.

Data retention

We keep waitlist information until openmat.me launches and for a reasonable period afterward, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@openmat.me and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any message, or by contacting us.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards to protect your information during such transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as openmat.me develops. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@openmat.me.
